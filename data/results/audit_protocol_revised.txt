REVISED EVALUATION AUDIT
Calibration positions excluded from binary metrics and ROC: 1-5
Monitoring starts at sequence position: 6
Binary metrics use only labels 0 and 1; transition label -1 is excluded.
Persistence window: W=2.
MLT boundary: floor(0.80 * sequence length); no confirmed alarm contributes 0.
MLT is an operational lead-to-boundary score, not remaining useful life or time to physical failure.
ROC CSVs must be regenerated by the *_ARTICLE_READY.py scripts; old ROC files are not valid after this correction.
AUC is calculated from continuous scores, not thresholded predictions.
Fine-tuning comparisons use paired per-drill F1 where both base and fine-tuned results are available.
Wilcoxon p-values are exploratory; Holm adjustment is applied within each sensing domain across available comparisons.
